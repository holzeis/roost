Roost — Privacy Policy

Effective 26 September 2026

Roost is a private, self-hosted chat, media-sharing, and video-calling app. It is not a commercial product or service: it is run by one person or family, for the exclusive use of the people they've personally invited, on infrastructure that person or family owns and controls. There is no company behind Roost collecting, processing, or profiting from your data.

This policy describes how the specific Roost instance you're using handles information. If someone other than the person listed at the bottom of this page invited you to use Roost, ask them directly how their instance is run — this document only speaks for this one.

Who can use this app

There is no public sign-up. The only way to use Roost is to already be a member of its operator's private Tailscale network. Your identity within the app is your Tailscale identity — there is no separate username, password, or account-creation flow, and no data is collected from anyone who hasn't been personally invited onto that network.

What information is collected

  • Identity — your Tailscale login name, plus a display name and profile photo you can set and change yourself.
  • Messages and media — text messages, photos, and videos you send, stored so they remain visible in your chat history.
  • Location shares — if you choose to share your live location in a chat, your coordinates are visible to the other people in that conversation until the share expires or you end it early.
  • Call metadata — who started a call, who was invited, and when it happened, so it can appear in chat history. Call audio/video itself is relayed live between participants and is not recorded or stored.
  • Delivery status — timestamps showing whether a message has been delivered to or seen by other participants.
  • Push notification tokens — if you enable notifications, a token identifying your device is stored so the server can wake the app for an incoming call or new message.

None of this is used for advertising, analytics, or profiling, and it is never sold or shared with any third party for marketing purposes.

Where information is stored

Everything above is stored on infrastructure the operator personally owns and controls — there is no third-party cloud database or analytics platform involved. The server is only reachable over the operator's private Tailscale network, never the public internet.

Third parties involved in delivering notifications

To wake the app for an incoming call or new message when it isn't actively running, Roost sends a push notification through Apple's Push Notification service (iOS) and Google's Firebase Cloud Messaging (iOS and Android). These are the only third parties involved anywhere in this app, and the notifications they carry contain no message content, location data, or media — only enough information to identify which chat to check, so the app can fetch the real content directly from the operator's own server.

How long information is kept

Messages and media are kept until you or another participant deletes them; there is no automatic expiry. Live location shares expire automatically at the time you set when sharing, or sooner if you end the share yourself. If you're removed from the operator's Tailscale network, you can no longer access the app, but message history you were part of remains visible to the other participants, the same as it would if you deleted the app yourself.

Children's privacy

Roost has no public sign-up and does not knowingly collect information from anyone outside the operator's personally-invited network, which may include their own family members of any age. It is not directed at children generally, and no data is ever collected from anyone the operator has not personally invited.

Security

All traffic to and from the server travels exclusively over Tailscale's encrypted (WireGuard) private network — the server has no listener on the public internet at all.

Changes to this policy

If how this instance handles information changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top revised accordingly.
